About

I am a Quantitative Analyst at EY in New York, in Quantitative Advisory Services. I build and validate credit risk models for large U.S. bank portfolios, covering CECL, CCAR stress testing and SR 11-7 model validation. I hold an M.S. in Statistics from Western Michigan University and a B.A. in Mathematics and Statistics from the University of Ghana. I also enjoy teaching statistics to non-technical audiences.
